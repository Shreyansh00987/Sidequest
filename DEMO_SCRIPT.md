# Sidequest: 60-Second Video Demo Script

This script is synchronized to real, working functionality in Sidequest. It is formatted with visual shot descriptions, narrator voiceover, and on-screen cues.

---

## Shot-by-Shot Timeline (0:00 – 1:00)

### [0:00 – 0:08] The Hook: The Outdoor Paradox
* **Visual**: Wide shot of a family walking along a park trail. A 7-year-old child looks bored, trailing behind looking down at the ground. Cut to phone showing Sidequest’s clean field notebook interface with zero social feeds or notifications.
* **Voiceover**: 
  > “Every parent knows the scene: you take your kids outside to touch grass, but within ten minutes, they’re bored. Yet handing them a digital attention trap defeats the purpose of being outdoors.”

---

### [0:08 – 0:18] The Product: One Clue at a Time
* **Visual**: Close-up of the Sidequest Welcome screen. The parent taps *"Backyard & Garden Explorer"*. The Briefing screen appears with safety rules and the green *"Ready Offline"* badge. Clue 1 appears in bold, daylight-readable type:  
  *“Find something rough that has stood here longer than you.”*  
  The phone is put into a pocket as the child runs toward an oak tree.
* **Voiceover**: 
  > “Sidequest is different. Its core principle: the screen should be the shortest part of the walk. One clue at a time. The phone gives the clue—the real world gives the answer.”

---

### [0:18 – 0:35] The Signature Experience: The Proof & The Blaze
* **Visual**: The child spots rough tree bark. The parent taps *"Take Proof Photo"*. The camera viewfinder opens. Snap!  
  Immediately, **The Blaze** trail-marker fills vertically from 0% to 100% in a smooth moss green.  
  *“Discovery Confirmed!”* appears along with a subtle haptic vibration and an educational field note about tree bark protection. Cut briefly to the adult diagnostic drawer showing a **+0.452 contrast margin** running in **43 ms**.
* **Voiceover**: 
  > “This isn't a mock-up. Sidequest runs a genuine open vision-language model right in the browser via WebAssembly. The Blaze fills upward based on real target-versus-distractor evidence—in just 43 milliseconds, completely on-device.”

---

### [0:35 – 0:43] Real Edge Cases: Non-Matches & Adult Override
* **Visual**: The next clue asks for green moss. The child accidentally snaps a photo of an asphalt path.  
  The Blaze indicates *"Keep looking"*, showing a gentle *“Try Again”* card explaining that asphalt doesn’t match moss. Cut to an ambiguous shady photo where the parent taps *“Count it!”* to keep the adventure moving without frustration.
* **Voiceover**: 
  > “When a discovery doesn't match or the light is tricky, it encourages another try—or lets parents tap ‘Count it!’ because real-world exploration is about curiosity, not rigid algorithms.”

---

### [0:43 – 0:51] The Artifact: The Private Keepsake
* **Visual**: The 5-clue hunt finishes. The screen reveals the **Field Naturalist Keepsake**: a beautifully formatted journal featuring the child's actual photos, timestamps, and nature notes. The parent taps *"Print Field Journal"*.
* **Voiceover**: 
  > “At the end of the walk, the child returns to a private keepsake journal of their real discoveries—ready to print or save, with zero cloud uploads.”

---

### [0:51 – 1:00] The Open AI Advantage
* **Visual**: Fast montage showing the phone in Airplane mode running smooth inference, the settings screen showing local IndexedDB storage, and the open-source GitHub repository. Final hero title: **Sidequest — The phone gives the clue. The world gives the answer.**
* **Voiceover**: 
  > “Open-source AI makes this possible: 100% offline in nature reserves, zero cloud API bills, and total privacy for your children's photos. Sidequest: built for the Touch Grass hackathon.”
